## 📱 Core Features

- **Salary tracking** — income entry, variable income support
- **Expense categorization** — Necessity, Health, Fun, Investment, Charity, Savings
- **Savings goals** — target amounts, progress tracking
- **Streak tracking** — daily engagement habits
- **Future planning** — month-by-month projections
- **Category allocations** — percentage-based budget rules
